# emnify ICP rubric

Who we want: companies that build or operate their own cellular-connected devices
and would benefit from one global SIM and platform instead of a carrier per country.

Starting hypothesis, to be calibrated against closed-won deals: 50 to 2,000 employees,
HQ in Europe or the US, hardware plus a platform rather than pure software.

## Gate

The company must sell or operate a device that connects over cellular
(2G, 3G, 4G/LTE, LTE-M, NB-IoT or 5G). If not: score 0, stop.

## Disqualifiers (score 0)

- Mobile operators and IoT connectivity resellers (competitors or partners)
- Software with no devices of its own
- Products that only use Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
- Fewer than 20 employees
- Already an emnify customer (flag for the account manager instead)

## Signals and points

| Signal | Why it matters | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device volume (installed base or units shipped) | Revenue grows with connected devices | 0 to 25 |
| Multi-country deployment or sells in the US | One SIM for 195+ countries; emnify is pushing into the US | 15 |
| Open embedded, firmware or IoT connectivity roles | A new device generation or bigger rollout is coming | 15 |
| Product launch or funding in the last 12 months | Money and a reason to scale now | 10 |
| Legacy 2G or 3G devices, network sunset | Forced migration is a buying trigger | 10 |
| Mentions eSIM, eUICC, SGP.32 or remote provisioning | emnify launched SGP.32 eSIM in March 2026 | 10 |
| Names a current IoT connectivity provider | Displacement play; tells the SDR what they walk into | 5 |

Device volume: under 1,000 = 5; 1,000 to 10,000 = 12; 10,000 to 100,000 = 20;
over 100,000 = 25; unknown = 0 (flag for the SDR to ask).

## Tiers

- A: 60 or more
- B: 40 to 59
- C: under 40

Only A and B go to Salesforce.
